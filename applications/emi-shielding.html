Application · In development

EMI Shielding

Sprayable nanotube coatings that absorb interference instead of reflecting it.

Absorb, do not reflect

A metal shield bounces interference back into the system. A nanotube network turns it into heat inside the coating.

  • Applied like paint, by spray or brush.
  • Carbon-based, so it adds little weight.
  • Layered: the surface lets waves in, the depth absorbs them.

How a project runs

  1. Tell us the band and the surface

    Frequency range, substrate, and your limits on thickness and weight.

  2. We design the layers

    The coating stack is modelled before anything is sprayed.

  3. Coupons, then measurements

    We coat test coupons and measure what they reflect and transmit.

Where this stands

This is a development programme, not a catalogue product. We publish no performance figures here: results depend on your band, substrate and thickness, and we report them only as measured.
