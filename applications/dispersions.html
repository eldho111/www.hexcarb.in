Application

Dispersions

Long nanotubes, held apart and evenly suspended. Ready to coat, print or mix.

Why this pathway

Dispersion quality strongly influences conductivity repeatability, film uniformity and manufacturability.

  • Match solvent and binder to your substrate.
  • Balance conductivity against viscosity.
  • Qualify storage and shear stability before pilot.

Material pathways

  1. Aqueous route

    Water-compatible, lower-VOC lines. Start with HexFlow A-Series.

  2. Solvent route

    Where resin or drying profile requires solvent. Start with HexFlow S-Series.

  3. Graphene dispersion route

    Barrier and printable coatings.
