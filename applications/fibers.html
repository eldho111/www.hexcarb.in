Application

Fibers

Aligned nanotubes, spun into conductive yarn.

Why this pathway

Performance depends on yarn architecture, contact design and cyclic durability.

  • Balance conductivity against bend radius, strain and fatigue.
  • Select yarn architecture for your integration method.
  • Plan connectors and environmental testing early.

Material pathways

  1. Sensing yarn route

    Strain monitoring where flex-cycle repeatability is critical.

  2. Conductive fiber route

    Low-mass current paths where metal can add weight.

  3. Tape route

    Directional reinforcement and conductive runs in laminates.
