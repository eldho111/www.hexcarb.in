    Why adoption is still catching up

    Seven families of carbon nanomaterials

    Each took a different path from lab to industry.

    Single-Wall Nanotubes

    Thin, highly conductive tubes for sensors and flexible electronics.

    Multi-Wall Nanotubes

    Adds conductivity and strength to plastics, coatings and shielding.

    Graphene & Graphene Oxide

    Atom-thin sheets for coatings, inks, thermal pads and composites.

    Fullerenes

    Carbon cages (C60), still used in electronics and specialty chemistry.

    Nanodiamond

    Ultra-hard nanoparticles for polishing and wear-resistant coatings.

    Conductive Carbon Black

    Adds electrical conductivity to plastics, rubber and industrial compounds.

    Carbon Nanofibers & Hybrids

    Combine conductivity, strength and processability in one system.
